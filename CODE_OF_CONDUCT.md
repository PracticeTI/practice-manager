# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in Practice Manager welcoming and
respectful for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not share sensitive or identifying
  information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
repository hosting service. Please do not include sensitive or identifying
information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports, protect the
reporter's confidentiality as far as practical, and may take any action
appropriate to keep the community safe, including removing content or limiting
participation.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code fairly and
consistently.
